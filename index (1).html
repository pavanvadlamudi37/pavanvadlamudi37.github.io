How to Play

1
Move Drag on the left side of the screen to move your character.
2
Shoot Tap the right side to fire your single bullet.
3
Recall Tap again to pull the bullet back when you need it.
4
Survive One touch from an enemy ends the run. Stay alive and hit the score target.
